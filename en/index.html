01The alliance

A shared table for thinking about AI and law.

GSAIL brings together scholars and institutions in conversation with colleagues around the world.

Its purpose is to strengthen Global South participation in AI governance, legal scholarship, research and public debate. That is why its table is deliberately shared between South and North: the questions and realities of the Global South are placed in dialogue with voices from other regions, so that the debate on AI and law can be genuinely global.

The DIAT Program and the Faculty of Law at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso (PUCV), Chile, organized the public launch of the alliance in Santiago on October 16, 2026.

03Areas of work

Four areas of work

  1. 01

    Research and knowledge production

    Legal research on artificial intelligence and its governance, grounded in the realities and questions of the Global South.

  2. 02

    Academic exchange and events

    Opportunities for scholars and institutions from different regions to meet and engage in dialogue.

  3. 03

    Publications: Global South Journal on AI & Law

    A publication line of the alliance, devoted to work on AI and law conceived from the Global South.

  4. 04

    Institutional cooperation and capacity-building

    Collaboration between institutions and capacity-building at the intersection of artificial intelligence and law.
